# About Open Civic Observatory

Open Civic Observatory documents public events through archives, data, timelines, and transparent analysis.

It is not a government agency, political organization, electoral campaign, or official news outlet.

## Principles

- Claims must be attributed to their sources.
- A statement is not automatically presented as fact.
- Contradictions are documented rather than hidden.
- Conclusions must identify the supporting evidence.
- Significant errors are corrected publicly.
- The privacy and safety of witnesses and relatives must be protected.
- Automation detects possible changes, but publication requires human review.

## First case

The observatory's first archive documents the death of Corey Ruiz and the public circulation of information related to the incident.
